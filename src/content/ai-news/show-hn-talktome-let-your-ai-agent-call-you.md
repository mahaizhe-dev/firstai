I built TalkToMe, an open-source macOS app that lets an agent call you from its current session. You answer, talk, and hear its replies. The agent keeps its tools, files, and history.It works with Codex (Tested extensively, quite smooth), Claude Code (not tested heavily), Hermes, and other agents that can run shell commands. You can use ElevenLabs or local models to power the conversation. It's really surreal to see your agent call you for something!
