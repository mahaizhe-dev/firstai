It can be difficult to get an overview of what's happening in AI. So I built a site that tracks AI-only news and announcements from 48 selected sources, including mainstream media, tech publications, bloggers, and company websites.On top of that, I've built in a mechanism that tracks how many of these outlets cover the same story: if it's mentioned by three or more, it qualifies as a trending story, which appears at the top of the site.It's also possible to get an email, when a new story starts trending.And there's also the option of getting a personalized daily AI brief of the subjects or entities one wants to follow.
