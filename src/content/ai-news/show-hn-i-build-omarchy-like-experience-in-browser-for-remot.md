I build Suwu, which inspired by Omarchy — the opinionated, keyboard-first Arch + Hyprland setup from DHH and team. With that same spirit, Suwu aims to bring that same level of enjoyment to the web-based remote shell to dev machine, with only one single binary as a super easy deployment. It starts from my own experiment making a plain xtermjs better, and as it has grown to be more enjoyable and much better I would like to share with you.
