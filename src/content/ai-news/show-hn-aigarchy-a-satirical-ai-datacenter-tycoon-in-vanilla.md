I built a short satirical browser game where you run an AI datacenter empire starting in November 2022.You scout power pockets and build clusters across the US, Silicon Valley, the EU, China, and eventually Low Earth Orbit — while chasing compute demand, taking circular financing from NVIDIA, lobbying Washington and Brussels, funding PACs in elections, and watching an in-game social feed react to every move. Can you find all 7 endings?Built in vanilla JS, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Audio (no build step, no dependencies), inspired by Paolo Pedercini's 2008 Flash game Oiligarchy. Works on desktop and mobile.
