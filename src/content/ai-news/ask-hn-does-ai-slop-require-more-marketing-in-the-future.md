Now that after Anthropic's IPO debut and with OpenAPI IPO in the works there is no doubt that the % of AI slop is going to increase disproportionately though this study says it has indeed gone down in 2026 since 2025 (https://tom-riley.co.uk/blog/ai-generated-content-statistics/) [The study itself seems to be an AI slop?] .An analysis of 100 companies highlighted on YouTube found that while AI generated content accounted for 52.1% of published material, it generated only 4.9% of organic traffic. Meanwhile, original human content made up just 14.5% of volume but captured 87% of the traffic. If this is the case then how can AI labs generate revenue?Even if we assume that the AI slop is going to raise after the IPOs, the content market is going to shrink and it will dilute the revenue for the content creators (using AI) forcing them to start marketing their AI slop to get visibility in the market. This is a very interesting trend to watch out for.When I say AI slop content I am not meaning just about web publishing (Web slop), but also synthetic vibe coding (Code slop), data analysis (Data slop), agentic workflow (Software Engineering slop) etc.If there are 1000 of the same kind of software/app clones (of course vibe coded) which will you buy? How does this compete with the originally human architected, designed and developed software/app? Finally I think it comes down to trust.Please share your thoughts.
