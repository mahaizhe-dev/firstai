Capitalism as a system of profit-seeking agents can't handle AI right. There is a very simple feedback loop:1. Agents seek to increase their profit by automating people.2. Each agent acts to its own good but at the same moment, the ultimate goal is to get rid of all consumers of what agents produce, because jobless people are very bad at consuming goods and services.3. Agents don't have any motivation to provide goods and services to consumers if they don't get anything valuable in return.Conclusion is simple. Either:a. AI development will be completely regulated and jobs will be preserved (thus negating the promised AI effectiveness), orb.
