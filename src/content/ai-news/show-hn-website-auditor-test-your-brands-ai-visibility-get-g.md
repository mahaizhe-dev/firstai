Hey HN community - Website Auditor crawls and hits 4 AI Assistants (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) based on your website's detected business sector with questions based on the market served. If your business comes back in the top five of your market served for that business sector, you are scored. If not, you get a 0. Based on that, I pull the citations AI assistants use to determine ranking, and put together a 90-day growth plan (available with a free trial subscription) for your site. It also checks the standard performance, security functions for your website. Intended audience is small business/solo-entrepreneur that cannot afford a marketing budget or paying Ahrefs/Semrush but needs to get traction for their business idea. Happy to answer any questions on the architecture or data. Also available as a chrome extension (AI Visibility Score) and MCP. Any feedback appreciated!
