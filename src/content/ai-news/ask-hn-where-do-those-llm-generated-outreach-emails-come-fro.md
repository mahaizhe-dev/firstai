The authors are real people with LinkedIn profiles, usually AI gurus, rarely experts in their niche. The message is usually targeted compliment > pitch > call to action. The compliment is the biggest tell because it's so neat and specific.My question: which tool are they all using? Why has it become so popular almost overnight? How do I reliably block those emails?
