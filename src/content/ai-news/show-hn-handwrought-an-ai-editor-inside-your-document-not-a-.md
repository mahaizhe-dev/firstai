I write a lot, and I got tired of the ChatGPT-in-a-second-tab shuffle: paste a paragraph in, describe the change, copy the answer back, lose track of what actually changed. So I built an editor where the AI suggestions show up in place — you review and accept/reject them like a diff, instead of reconciling a wall of chat text by hand.It's a chat panel next to a real editor. Ask for a rewrite, a tighter opening, a different tone — the suggestion lands as an inline, reversible edit, not a new chat bubble to copy out.Guest mode gets you 5 generations with no signup, so you can try it cold. Built solo in 5 weeks, deployed, and never shown to anyone outside me until right now — so it's rough in a few honest ways: credits (one-time purchase, no subscription) are built but still waitlist-only until launch day.Would love feedback, especially on where the "review before it's yours" flow feels slow or gets in the way rather than helping.
