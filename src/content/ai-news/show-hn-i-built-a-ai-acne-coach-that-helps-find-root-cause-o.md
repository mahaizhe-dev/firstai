I’ve had acne for 10 years and I'm so exhausted of dont know what happened. So, I'm building AcneAlly to learn you overtime to figure out what's causing your acne, and to give personalized support. Instead of tracking your skin or logging daily habits, you can just talk or interact with it like a companion. You can share about your acne, share your concerns, or ask anything you want. Based on what you share, it uses Al to reasoning to make guesses about what could be causing your acne. As you share more, it update it understanding abt you, its guesses abt the cause and it suggestions. This is an early version, and there's still a lot to improve. I'd love to hear your feedback!
