Hey everyone, I'm Steven. I wanted to share a new project we're working on called Wyrd. It's a real-time verification platform for AI systems. Whether it's AI or standard services, every team uses a variety of tools to determine if their systems are working as expected. However, there tends to be a dearth of tools/platforms that can do this in real-time. From our perspective, as AI is used more and more to make autonomous decisions, there should be more emphasis on real-time verification. So we built Wyrd, which provides you and your teams all of the primitives to build your own real-time verification workflows.
