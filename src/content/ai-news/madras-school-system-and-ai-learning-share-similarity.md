Madras School education system (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_School) that was the bedrock of mass public structured education in the late 18-19th century has several similarities on how AI is trained today.They both ensures that knowledge dissemination is free.The similarities they share are as follows1) Madras school & AI distillation (Teacher-Student models) encourages Students to teach others instead of just teachers thereby reducing cost.2) Madras school & Reinforcement learning rewards or awards penalty scores based on the assessment/feedback.3) Both systems ultimately ended in rote learning and hallucinations.The other day when I noticed that several leading professors of top universities were sharing their top courses for free on YT. I immediately remembered our education system (Madras School) and how it had a profound impact in today's education system especially public mass elementary schooling system. It is also notable that today's AI training took a leaf out of it. Those professors were indeed trying to bring Madras school system to the Universities by free knowledge dissemination that too in this AI era, which is commendable.But the current commercial education system is a stark difference from the Madras school which was designed for the mass and was affordable. But today's education and research is full of competition and highly unaffordable which was not the case in the early 19th century.Finally, I would like to conclude that the commercial education (training teachers) and proprietary SOTA frontier models both gate keep their highest tier capabilities behind high tuition or API paywalls, whereas open weights models and the Madras System prioritize broad, low cost knowledge dissemination.
