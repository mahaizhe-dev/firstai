I have been seeing tons of stuff about people developing 'second brains' as a data vault for their AI usage on X.I fully understand the ideology, in terms of storing data, reducing context, having a single pane to work off of, etc.The part I struggle with, is that as an employee, I feel like every time I create a workflow, recurring data-pull, etc., it feels as if I am creating an app that I have to maintain myself. When my organization is constantly changing permissions, MCP accessibility, and token usage, I am having to maintain the app as if I were a developer, updating the automations as time goes on.I am grateful that I am technical enough to do this, and could probably avoid much of this problem if I had better technical foresight, but for a non-technical employee this seems like a significant barrier to AI-success on a per-employee basis.Anybody else experience the same maintenance struggle? I have developed countless useful agents that just die off in my mind after a few days because I forget about them, and they break.
