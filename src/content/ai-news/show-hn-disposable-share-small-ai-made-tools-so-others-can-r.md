I made a site for sharing small tools made with AI.With agents, it is now quick to build a tool that fits your own task. But those tools usually get thrown away after use, and the next person who may needs something similar start again from zero. So I thought maybe if result or process could shared by text or code and this can lead to kind of token sharing economy. Its name is Disposable and compared to "Open source", I named its philosophy to "Open Disposable".So the principle is "Like open source, without the upkeep. Keep the know-how, rebuild the tool your way". You can share anything you actually used and found useful, with no promise to maintain it. Others can use it as is, or give the spec that comes with it to theur own agent and have it rebuilt.If you have your own skill, prompt, or program you would like to show others, please share it.
