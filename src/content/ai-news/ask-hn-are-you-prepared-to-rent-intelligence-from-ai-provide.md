Say the year 2035 arrives, and for the last ten years you have relied on an AI machine to do your thinking for you because you convinced yourself it made you more productive. As a consequence, your native intelligence has atrophied, and you now depend on this synthetic pseudo-brain the way a diabetic depends on insulin after the pancreas has failed.Then AI providers put a tollbooth on thinking. What happens if you can't afford to rent your thinking? You are left with the intelligence of a farm animal, because your natural brain has become useless.
