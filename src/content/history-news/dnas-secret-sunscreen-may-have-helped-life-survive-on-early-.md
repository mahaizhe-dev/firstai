Scientists have uncovered a surprising reason why early life may have survived Earth's intense ultraviolet radiation: a key component of DNA appears to turn potentially destructive damage into something easier to repair. The discovery could help solve a decades-old evolutionary mystery and reveal how life endured the harsh conditions of our planet's earliest days.
