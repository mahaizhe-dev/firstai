A new theory suggests that the brain may chemically encode and retrieve memories rather than relying solely on electrical signals. Researchers propose that interactions between brain cells and their molecular surroundings could help explain the intimate connection between memory, emotion, and thought. Their findings challenge the assumption that increasingly powerful computers could eventually develop human-like consciousness.
