NASA’s Voyager 1 has reached an extraordinary milestone, traveling 16.1 billion miles from Earth to become the first spacecraft so distant that its signals take a full day to reach us. Nearly 50 years after launch, the aging probe continues its historic journey through interstellar space, pushing the limits of human exploration despite dwindling power and technical challenges.
