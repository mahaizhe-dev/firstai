Scientists have discovered that psychopathic traits are associated with surprisingly different patterns of language, with some individuals using more positive words and others expressing greater negativity or less anxiety. The findings challenge familiar stereotypes about psychopathy and suggest that the stories people tell about their lives may offer important clues about their personalities.
