The human family tree may need a major overhaul. New research from Monash University challenges the traditional classification of our ancient ancestors, arguing that species such as Australopithecus and Paranthropus should be grouped within our own genus, Homo. As scientists uncover more fossils and analyze evolutionary relationships, the boundaries that once separated these ancient relatives are becoming increasingly difficult to defend.
