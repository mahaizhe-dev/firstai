Deep beneath the Pacific Ocean, scientists have discovered tiny hidden ecosystems inside metal-rich rocks that take millions of years to form. Researchers examined 244 nodules and found more than 4,100 animals living in their cracks and cavities, including unique communities unlike those in the surrounding seafloor mud. These remarkable miniature habitats could now be threatened by deep-sea mining for valuable metals.
