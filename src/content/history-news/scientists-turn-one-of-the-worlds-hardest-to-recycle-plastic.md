Virginia Tech scientists have discovered how to transform one of the world's hardest-to-recycle plastics into a valuable ingredient for high-performance engine oil. Their innovative process converts discarded PVC into a useful lubricant material in just three hours. The breakthrough could turn a major source of plastic pollution into a valuable industrial resource while making lubricant production more sustainable.
