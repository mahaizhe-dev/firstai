A major breakthrough in superconductivity research has revealed that one of the essential ingredients for electricity to flow without resistance can exist at room temperature. Scientists discovered that electrons can form stable pairs at much higher temperatures than previously understood, suggesting that a different obstacle may be preventing room-temperature superconductivity.
