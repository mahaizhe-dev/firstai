More than 300 million years ago, long before dinosaurs roamed Earth, the Chicago area was a steamy, swampy landscape teeming with bizarre creatures. Now, scientists at the Field Museum have identified a previously unrecognized species of ancient amphibian ancestor, revealing new clues about the evolution of frogs and salamanders.
