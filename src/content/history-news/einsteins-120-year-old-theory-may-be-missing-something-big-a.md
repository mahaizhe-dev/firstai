A surprising discovery by MIT and UC Santa Barbara physicists reveals that Einstein’s famous theory of particle motion misses something important when particles travel near the speed of light. Their new calculations show that heavy quarks moving through an ultra-hot plasma experience rare but unusually powerful jolts that Einstein’s traditional model cannot fully explain.
