The deep sea has long been considered a safe haven for marine life, but new genetic research suggests a hidden biodiversity crisis may be unfolding far beneath the waves. Scientists analyzing more than a decade of genetic data from five species of deep-sea fish and shrimp in the Gulf discovered troubling signs of extinction risk in every species examined.
