Quite a niche project that shows in a neat way historical wind force and orientation, anywhere on earth. No particular use case in mind, apart from checking that my next house will not be affected by the Mistral wind (the horrendous wind displayed in the default coordinates of the app). Based on weather reanalysis of ERA5, ERA5-LAND and ECMWF IFS provided by open-meteo.com Entirely Vibe coded with DeepSeek 4.1 Flash and Opencode.
