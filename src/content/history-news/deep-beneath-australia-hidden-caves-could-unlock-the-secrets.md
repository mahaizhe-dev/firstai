Scientists have solved the mystery of strange trenches scattered across Australia’s vast Nullarbor Plain, revealing an extensive hidden world beneath the surface. These subtle landscape features are created as deeply buried cave systems slowly collapse upward over millions of years. The discovery could help researchers locate unexplored caves containing ancient environmental records and unusual life forms.
