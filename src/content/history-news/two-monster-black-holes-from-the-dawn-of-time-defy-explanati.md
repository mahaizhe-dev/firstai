Astronomers have discovered the oldest known compact pair of quasars, powered by two enormous black holes that existed just 900 million years after the Big Bang. Weighing an astonishing 1.5 billion and 400 million times the mass of the Sun, these cosmic giants somehow grew to incredible sizes far earlier than current theories can explain.
