Scientists have discovered a strange bacterium hiding beneath the skin of deep-sea creatures known as sea pigs, revealing an unexpected biological partnership. The microbe has an unusually tiny genome, yet more than a quarter of its DNA is devoted to making surface proteins, some of them exceptionally large. Researchers suspect it depends heavily on its host for survival, but what the sea pigs gain in return remains a mystery.
