Astronomers have traced mysterious gas clouds near the Milky Way's central supermassive black hole to a massive pair of stars whose colliding winds appear to create dense clumps of material. These clouds may eventually feed the black hole, revealing a surprising connection between stellar activity and the growth of cosmic giants.
