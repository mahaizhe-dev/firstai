A major new investigation into Stonehenge’s mysterious six-ton Altar Stone has uncovered fresh clues while ruling out several suspected sources. Once thought to have originated in Wales, the massive sandstone slab is now believed to have come from northeastern Scotland, although researchers have yet to find an exact match.
