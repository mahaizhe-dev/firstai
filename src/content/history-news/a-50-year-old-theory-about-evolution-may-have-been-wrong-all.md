A decades-old theory suggesting that evolution protects its most important genes from mutations has been dealt a major blow. An extensive new analysis of mutations in bacteria and yeast found no evidence that essential genes mutate less frequently than other genes. Researchers say earlier studies may have been misled by harmful mutations that disappeared through natural selection before scientists could detect them.
