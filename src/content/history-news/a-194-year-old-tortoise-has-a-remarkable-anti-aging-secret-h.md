At an astonishing 194 years old, Jonathan the giant tortoise is the world's oldest living land animal, and scientists may have finally uncovered the secrets behind his extraordinary lifespan. Researchers identified 287 unique genetic variants linked to processes that help repair damaged DNA, control inflammation, regulate insulin, and suppress cancer.
