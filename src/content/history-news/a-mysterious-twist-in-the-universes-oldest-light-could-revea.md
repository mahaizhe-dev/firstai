Scientists have spotted hints of a mysterious rotation in the universe’s oldest light that could reveal new physics and shed light on dark matter and dark energy. A new technique developed at UC San Diego could help rule out misleading telescope errors, bringing researchers closer to determining whether the strange signal is real.
