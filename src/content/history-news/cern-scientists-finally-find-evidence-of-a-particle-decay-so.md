Scientists at CERN have found the first evidence of an incredibly rare particle decay after more than 40 years of searching, detecting a process expected to occur just once in every 10 billion beauty particles. The breakthrough could help physicists explore a mysterious microscopic realm where unknown particles and forces may be hiding.
