Scientists have successfully transmitted a message through a brick wall using nothing but gravity, an old elevator, and an incredibly sensitive detector. By moving a massive counterweight between two positions, researchers encoded the word "Gravity" into tiny changes in gravitational pull. Unlike Wi-Fi or radio signals, gravity passes through ordinary barriers without being blocked, offering a potentially revolutionary way to communicate.
