ESA’s Euclid space telescope has discovered its first previously unknown comet, despite being designed to study distant galaxies and the mysteries of the dark Universe. The icy object is approximately 1.3 billion kilometers from Earth and follows an unusual orbit that takes thousands of years to complete.
