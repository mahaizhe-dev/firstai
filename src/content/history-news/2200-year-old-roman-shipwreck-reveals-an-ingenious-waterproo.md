A 2,200-year-old Roman shipwreck has revealed sophisticated waterproofing techniques involving pine tar and beeswax. Microscopic pollen grains trapped in the ancient coatings are now helping scientists reconstruct where the vessel may have traveled and undergone repairs more than two millennia ago.
