Scientists have discovered that vitamin C may help prevent potentially cancer-causing chemicals from forming in the stomach after eating foods containing nitrates and nitrites. A new mathematical model suggests that taking vitamin C after meals could reduce these harmful reactions, offering a possible new strategy for lowering cancer risk.
