I made a site that lets you see 17,000+ battles and wars across all recorded history on an interactive globe. Main features:- Auto-play to see a timelapse of conflicts spanning 2600 BCE to present day- Select any war to step through its battles chronologically- Manually scrub the timeline or search for specific events or time periodsData is sourced from Wikidata (with data cleaned and events categorized in an offline pipeline) and Wikipedia (for loading summaries and event stats in real-time). These are amazing resources but coverage is thinner for non-Western history. You might also notice that the timeline isn’t linear; this is to counterbalance the sparsity of recorded events as you look further back in time.I’d love to get your feedback! Is it informative, interesting, fun? Is the UX intuitive (particularly for entering, using, and exiting “War Focus mode”)? Thanks!
