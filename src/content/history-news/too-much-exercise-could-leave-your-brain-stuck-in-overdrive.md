Exercising too much without adequate recovery may disrupt brain chemistry in surprising ways. Scientists found that mice subjected to prolonged, intense training had elevated dopamine levels in a brain region linked to memory and emotions, even 48 hours after exercising. The discovery could help explain why overtrained athletes sometimes struggle with insomnia, anxiety, and persistent fatigue, although these symptoms were not directly measured.
