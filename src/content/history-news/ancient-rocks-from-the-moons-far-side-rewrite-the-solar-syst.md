Scientists studying ancient rocks from the Moon’s far side have uncovered surprising evidence that challenges decades of thinking about the Solar System’s violent past. Instead of a single catastrophic bombardment, asteroid impacts appear to have declined gradually over billions of years, revealing new clues about Earth's early evolution and the origins of life.
