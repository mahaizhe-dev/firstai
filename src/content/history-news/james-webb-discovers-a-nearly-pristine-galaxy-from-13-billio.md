Astronomers have discovered an extraordinarily primitive galaxy from 13 billion years ago that may carry the chemical fingerprints of the universe’s first stars. The discovery could solve a decades-old mystery surrounding ancient galaxies near the Milky Way and reveal how the building blocks of life first spread across the cosmos.
