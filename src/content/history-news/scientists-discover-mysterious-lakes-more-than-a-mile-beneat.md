More than a mile beneath the surface of the Red Sea, scientists have discovered rare underwater lakes that could reveal secrets about Earth's ancient oceans and the possibility of alien life. Known as brine pools, these extremely salty, oxygen-free environments are surprisingly teeming with microbial life, challenging our understanding of where living organisms can survive.
