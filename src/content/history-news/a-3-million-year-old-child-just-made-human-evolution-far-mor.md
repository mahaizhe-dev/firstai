A groundbreaking study of the 3-million-year-old Taung Child fossil suggests that several ancient human ancestors once thought to belong to the same species may actually represent different branches of our evolutionary family tree. The discovery challenges a century of assumptions and reveals how dramatic climate changes helped shape a surprisingly complex story of human origins.
