Medications taken years or even long ago may leave lasting changes in your gut microbiome, according to a study of more than 2,500 people. Researchers discovered that common drugs, including antidepressants, blood pressure medications, and anxiety treatments, can leave microbial “fingerprints” long after patients stop taking them. Surprisingly, some anxiety medications had effects comparable to broad-spectrum antibiotics.
