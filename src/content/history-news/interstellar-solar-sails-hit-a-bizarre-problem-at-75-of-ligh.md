Scientists have discovered a surprising obstacle to interstellar travel: at extreme speeds, the light propelling futuristic solar sails could actually begin slowing them down. Once a spacecraft reaches 75% of the speed of light, some scattered light starts producing drag, potentially complicating humanity’s plans to reach distant stars.
