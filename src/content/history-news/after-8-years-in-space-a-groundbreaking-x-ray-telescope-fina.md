After eight years and 10 billion kilometers in space, a groundbreaking X-ray telescope heading to Mercury has captured its first cosmic observations and is performing even better than scientists expected. The instrument could soon reveal Mercury's hidden chemical secrets and transform our understanding of how the solar system's smallest planet formed.
