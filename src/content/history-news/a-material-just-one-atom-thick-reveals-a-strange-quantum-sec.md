Researchers at the University of Osaka have discovered a remarkable quantum state forming at the boundary between a one-atom-thick material and a metal. They observed electrons behaving as though they were unusually heavy, including a previously unseen state created by interactions between the ultrathin layer and the copper beneath it. This first-of-its-kind observation reveals a promising new way to control exotic quantum behavior.
