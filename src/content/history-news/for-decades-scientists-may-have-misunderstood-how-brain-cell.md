A major new study challenges a long-standing assumption about how the brain works, revealing that most neurons are versatile multitaskers rather than highly specialized workers. By analyzing recordings from 43 regions of the mouse brain, researchers discovered that although some neurons are dedicated to specific functions, most respond to a wide variety of information in remarkably distinct ways.
