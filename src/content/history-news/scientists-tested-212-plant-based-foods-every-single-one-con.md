A new study has uncovered a surprising concern hiding in popular plant-based foods and drinks. Researchers tested 212 vegan and vegetarian products sold in the UK, including meat alternatives, oat milk, almond milk, and soy milk, and found that every single one contained at least one potentially harmful fungal toxin. Known as mycotoxins, these naturally occurring compounds can contaminate grains, legumes, and seeds during farming and storage.
