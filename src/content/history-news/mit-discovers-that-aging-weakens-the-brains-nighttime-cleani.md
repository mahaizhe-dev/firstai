MIT researchers have discovered that the brain’s natural waste-removal system becomes significantly less effective during sleep as people age. The decline is linked to weaker deep-sleep brain waves and changes in blood flow, potentially offering new clues about why aging increases the risk of Alzheimer's disease and dementia. Surprisingly, some older adults retain the same powerful cleansing waves seen in younger people.
