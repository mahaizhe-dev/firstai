A bizarre whale that lived roughly 6 million years ago had a face so dramatically crooked that scientists believe it may have been the most asymmetrical of any mammal. Discovered in Peru, the extinct pygmy sperm whale Scaphokogia huachafo had an enormous downward-curving snout and a skull so distorted that researchers initially thought it had been damaged after death. Instead, its strange anatomy appears to have been an extraordinary evolutionary adaptation for hunting along the seafloor.
