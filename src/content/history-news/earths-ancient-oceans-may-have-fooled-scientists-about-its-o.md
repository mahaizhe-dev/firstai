Earth’s journey from an oxygen-starved world to a planet capable of supporting complex life may have unfolded very differently than scientists once believed. A new analysis suggests that oxygen accumulated in the atmosphere in three major stages, with the oceans taking millions or even hundreds of millions of years to catch up.
