The narwhal’s iconic spiral tusk, once prized in Europe as the horn of a mythical unicorn, conceals an extraordinary feat of natural engineering. Using advanced X-ray technology, an international team of scientists discovered that the tusk contains two intertwined microscopic spirals that twist in opposite directions.
