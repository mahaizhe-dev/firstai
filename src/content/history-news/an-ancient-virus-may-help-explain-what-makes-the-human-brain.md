A virus that infected chimpanzees millions of years ago may have helped shape one of the most important differences between humans and our closest relatives: how our brains develop. Scientists at Lund University discovered that ancient viral DNA embedded in the chimpanzee genome switches off a gene called LINC00662, which remains active in humans and appears to play an important role in early brain development.
