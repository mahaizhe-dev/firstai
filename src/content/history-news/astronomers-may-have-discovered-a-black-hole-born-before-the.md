A mysterious object named Phoebe, detected when it briefly magnified a distant star, could be a primordial black hole formed just after the Big Bang. With an estimated mass only three times that of the Moon, it may be a 13-billion-year-old cosmic relic that offers new clues to the mystery of dark matter.
