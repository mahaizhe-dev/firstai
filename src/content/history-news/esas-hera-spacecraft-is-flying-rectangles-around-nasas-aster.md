Europe’s Hera spacecraft has arrived at the scene of NASA’s historic asteroid collision to uncover what really happened when a spacecraft slammed into Dimorphos in 2022. Its close-up investigation could reveal whether the impact left a crater or reshaped the asteroid entirely, providing crucial clues for protecting Earth from future threats.
