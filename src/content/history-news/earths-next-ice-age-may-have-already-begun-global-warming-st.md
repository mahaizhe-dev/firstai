Scientists have uncovered surprising evidence that Earth’s next ice age may have already begun, only to be stopped by global warming. By studying ancient plants preserved beneath the Barnes Ice Cap in the Canadian Arctic, researchers discovered that the remnants of a massive ice sheet began expanding again about 2,000 years ago, signaling the possible start of a new glacial period. But by the 1960s, rising temperatures had reversed that growth, causing the ice to shrink instead.
