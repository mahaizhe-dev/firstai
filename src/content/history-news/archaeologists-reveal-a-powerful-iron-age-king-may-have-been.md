Archaeologists have uncovered evidence that Verica, the Iron Age ruler whose exile helped trigger Rome’s invasion of Britain in AD 43, was actually a woman. The discovery challenges centuries of historical assumptions and suggests that a largely forgotten female leader may have changed the course of British history.
