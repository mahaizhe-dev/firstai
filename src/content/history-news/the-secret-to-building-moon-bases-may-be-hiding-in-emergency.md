Scientists have turned the thin plastic used in emergency blankets into remarkably strong insulation that could help astronauts survive the Moon's extreme temperatures. Built from hundreds of stacked layers, the material withstands crushing pressure without losing its thermal protection, potentially paving the way for tougher, longer-lasting lunar habitats.
