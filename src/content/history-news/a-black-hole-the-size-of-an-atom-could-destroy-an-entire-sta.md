MIT scientists have discovered that tiny black holes left over from the Big Bang could slowly devour stars from within, eventually triggering spectacular explosions visible across galaxies. Detecting these unusual cosmic events could reveal the existence of primordial black holes and help solve the mystery of dark matter.
