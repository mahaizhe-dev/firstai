A surprising discovery suggests the Milky Way may be making stars in places where most galaxies cannot. Scientists found evidence that a massive rotating structure near the galactic center is creating powerful shock waves that trigger star formation rather than suppress it. Even more unexpectedly, part of the Norma spiral arm may not be a spiral arm at all, but a vast region where new stars are being born.
