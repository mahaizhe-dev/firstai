A remarkable 125,000-year-old discovery in Saudi Arabia reveals that early humans once thrived beside enormous lakes filled with wildlife in what is now a vast desert. Thousands of stone tools and ancient plant remains suggest these prehistoric people used sophisticated hunting techniques, potentially including weapons enhanced with poisonous plant compounds.
