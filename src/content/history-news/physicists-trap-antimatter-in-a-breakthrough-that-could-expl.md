Positrons are the antimatter counterpart of an electron. Physicists trapped a positron and cooled it to temperatures near absolute zero. Positron is suspended in a wall-free trap, safely separated from ordinary matter. Team will conduct most precise comparison yet between matter and antimatter. Any difference between the electron and positron could expose cracks in the Standard Model of particle physics.
