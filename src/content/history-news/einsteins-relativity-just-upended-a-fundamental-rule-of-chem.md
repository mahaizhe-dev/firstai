A surprising discovery at Brown University is challenging one of chemistry's most fundamental textbook explanations. Researchers found direct evidence that Einstein's theory of relativity changes how heavy atoms form triple bonds, causing normally distinct types of chemical bonds to blend together. The breakthrough confirms a decades-old prediction and suggests that traditional chemistry rules don't fully apply to heavy elements such as bismuth.
