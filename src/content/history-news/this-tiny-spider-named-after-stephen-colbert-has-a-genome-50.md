A tiny California trapdoor spider named after Stephen Colbert has a genome roughly 50% larger than a human's, despite being only about the size of a quarter. Scientists discovered that its enormous genome contains 4.6 billion DNA base pairs and 52 chromosomes, revealing surprising clues about spider evolution.
