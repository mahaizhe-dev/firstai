Scientists have discovered an unusual carbon signature in Hawaiian volcanic rocks that suggests some of Earth's carbon originated in ancient meteorites and later escaped from deep within the planet's core. The discovery raises an astonishing possibility: Carbon essential to life may have traveled from space to Earth's depths before emerging through volcanoes billions of years later.
